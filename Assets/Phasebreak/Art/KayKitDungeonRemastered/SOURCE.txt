Only selected free-tier FBX and required atlas textures are included. No paid EXTRA or SOURCE content.

Curated models and purpose:
- barrel_large, barrel_small_stack, crates_stacked: abandoned camp and roadside supply clutter.
- chair, table_long_broken: abandoned camp furniture.
- torch, torch_mounted: camp and shrine light fixtures; no light component is configured.
- wall_broken, wall_cracked, wall_arched, pillar_decorated: ruin and shrine landmark pieces for #145/#146.
The eleven selected FBXs share Textures/dungeon_texture.png. Source size is already suitable in Unity meters (barrels about 2 units high, walls 4 units high). Prefabs are visual-only and are not placed in a scene.
