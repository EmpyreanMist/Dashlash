Only selected free-tier FBX and required atlas textures are included. No paid EXTRA or SOURCE content.

Curated models and purpose:
- barrel, bucket_empty, crate_A_big, pallet, sack: road and camp supply clutter.
- target, weaponrack: abandoned training camp props.
- tent, wheelbarrow: roadside camp and transport props.
- rock_single_A, tree_single_A, tree_single_A_cut: landmark nature pieces for #145/#146.
These twelve FBXs share Textures/hexagons_medieval.png. Unity importer globalScale is 5 for this pack because the free-tier source models are small (for example, the tent is about 0.52 units high at source scale). The selected prefabs are visual-only and are not placed in a scene; hexagonal terrain tiles/buildings were not imported.
