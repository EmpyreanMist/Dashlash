# StarterZone_V2 implementation report

## World and MapMagic

- Scene: `Assets/Phasebreak/Scenes/StarterZone_V2.unity`, first in build settings. `Assets/dashlash.unity` remains second as a fallback.
- Four pinned 1000 m tiles cover (0, 0) to (2000, 2000). Heightmaps use 513 samples per side, splatmaps 512, and maximum terrain height 320 m.
- Graph: `Assets/Phasebreak/Data/StarterZoneV2/ShatteredFrontier.asset` with `FrontierTerrainNode -> HeightOutput200` and fixed-seed `Noise200 -> TexturesOutput200` over a grass base. `PHASEBREAK_STARTER_V2_SEED` is `271828`.
- The finishing pass paints grass, dirt, cliff, road and Rift ground. Terrain is generated by MapMagic in the Editor and fixed at runtime; the disabled MapMagic component and graph remain in the V2 scene for regeneration.

## Regions, roads, and landmarks

- Southwest starter pocket, woodland opening trail, introductory clearing, broken gate vista, Old King's Road and branches, Northgate, Westmere lowland, Eastwatch ridge, Ancient Ruins, Old Fort watchtower, Rift basin, Rift Crypt entrance, Old Mine hook, and northern/eastern/southern/Rift onward routes.
- Northgate has a palisade ring with an open southern entrance, cottages, two towers, market stalls, training yard, and the existing Warden, Quartermaster, Seer, and Guard. Westmere and Eastwatch have smaller sets of buildings and their original NPCs.
- Road surfaces are terrain paint rather than colliding mesh slabs. The terrain generator smooths the major route corridors. Sampled peak grades at 5 m intervals: opening/King's Road 7.7%, Westmere 4.3%, Eastwatch 5.1%, Rift road 13.6%.
- Biome treatment: conifer start, open central grassland, western lowlands, wooded belts, rocky northern highlands, and sparse dark Rift basin. No new water dependency was added.

## Combat and grinding

- `FrontierEncounterZone` defines 26 stable encounter centers: one two-enemy introductory pack, ordinary three/four-enemy packs, and five six-enemy grinding clearings. The full designed population is 99, but only zones within 230 m instantiate enemies; they unload beyond 300 m.
- Ordinary enemies return after 180 seconds; larger clearings after 240 seconds. Respawn waits while the player is within 48 m. The existing zombie prefab still handles leash, defeat, loot container, and XP. The current player progression code supports levels 1–10; V2 does not change that cap.
- Grinding clearings are concentrated near the Ancient Ruins, Eastwatch outskirts, and Rift approach, with smaller packs along side roads and near Westmere.

## Gameplay, performance, and tests

- Existing Player, camera, targeting, combat, abilities, inventory, progression, quest journal, six NPCs, HUD, and Rift Crypt logic were retained in the copied scene. V2 changes spawn and world positions. `WorldQuestHud` uses V2 map bounds and quest/landmark markers only while V2 is active.
- Play Mode: the Player spawned near (145, 155); queued W input walked the opening corridor to the ridge while remaining grounded. The first pocket activated two enemies. A scripted combat hit defeated one, advanced existing XP from level 3 with 46 XP to level 4 with 8 XP, and created its death state. A shortened cooldown test respawned that enemy after the player moved away. Saved progression and quest values were restored after this test.
- Play Mode: Warden Elira was discoverable and interactive in Northgate; Rift Crypt showed its entrance prompt and `TryInteract` entered the existing dungeon instance. Four terrain tiles and three surface layers persisted after a scene reload. Vista, Northgate, first combat pocket, and spawn were visually inspected in Game view.
- The one-command **Phasebreak > Rebuild Complete Starter Zone V2** workflow was run successfully after the final builder changes. The saved scene reloaded with four terrain tiles, three terrain layers, 13 Northgate cottages, one market/training square, disabled runtime MapMagic generation, and an 1800 m camera clip. It entered Play Mode with 26 encounter zones and no live console errors.
- Terrain tile expansion is disabled at runtime, pine materials use URP cutout rendering, and nearby encounter activation limits live enemy components. A sampled Editor Play view reported 1,238 draw calls and about 2.68 million triangles. These are Editor statistics, not a standalone performance verdict.

Game view QA captures: `STARTER_ZONE_V2_QA/v2-final-spawn.png`, `v2-final-combat.png`, `v2-skytest.png` (vista), and `v2-final-northgate.png`.

## Known issues

- Northgate architecture, watchtowers, ruins, and exit gates are functional layout art with simple geometry. The Game view still reads as a prototype, especially across empty settlement spaces and distant slopes. This is a real visual quality gap relative to the requested finished RPG look.
- Westmere is a lowland environment without a modeled water body. The Old Mine and future exits are geographic/visual hooks, with no destination scenes yet.
- The full quest chain, boss completion, corpse looting UI, and repeated grinding to level 10 were not exercised end to end. The world map UI was updated and compiled but not manually navigated in the Game view.
- The aerial Scene view capture did not render reliably through the connected Editor pipeline; Game view captures and terrain/route measurements were used for visual QA. A standalone build and device performance run remain outstanding.

The old scene and gameplay source remain as fallback. No remote push or local commit was made.
