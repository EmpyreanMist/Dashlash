# PHASEBREAK project state

Authoritative branch: main
Gameplay baseline verified: 2026-09-19

PHASEBREAK is a single-player Unity 6000.6.1f1 vertical slice. This document records implemented capability and verification limits; GitHub Issues hold actionable work.

## Playable world

- `Assets/Phasebreak/Scenes/StarterZone_V2.unity` is first in build settings. Its four fixed MapMagic terrain tiles cover a 2 km square with a southwest spawn, Northgate, Westmere, Eastwatch, Ancient Ruins, Rift Crypt entrance, six quest NPCs, eight authored world encounter sites, and a separate Riftblade practice lane. `Assets/dashlash.unity` remains the older fallback scene.
- V2 activates nearby encounter zones and respawns them on timers. Road stragglers, a broken-cart ambush, an abandoned camp, a brute guard, an imp ridge, a ruined shrine, an Elite watch, and the crypt approach use authored Zombie, Puglin Brute, Imp Skirmisher, Rift Caster, and Puglin Charger roles and offsets with Normal/Veteran/Elite ranks. Caster winds up a slower rift projectile and Charger commits to a telegraphed rush with recovery; Elite ranged roles add a second angled shot. The Fort and Crypt each have a named ranked enemy with a guaranteed corpse drop through the existing loot owner. The Riftblade practice lane remains zombies. Non-Zombie role stats and local color variants come from `EnemyRoleDefinition` assets. All roles keep the existing enemy, XP, and corpse-loot owners. `docs/reference/STARTER_ZONE_V2.md` describes world ownership, local asset import, and rebuilding.
- The V0.2 world pass gives Northgate, Westmere, and Eastwatch less regular house spacing, kit wagons/crates, supply yards, fences, banners, and a fortified Northgate entrance. Grouped pine edges and roadside details frame the routes; soft earth footpaths connect buildings without changing the MapMagic graph. Existing village kit assets are CC0. Scene dressing remains re-runnable through **Phasebreak > Author V0.2 Starter Zone World Quality**.

## Implemented systems and ownership

- `Assets/Phasebreak/Scripts` owns third-person movement, jump/air control, camera, targeting, interaction, five combat abilities, Energy, health/death, XP/levels 1–10, inventory/equipment, set effects, specialization, talents, quests, enemy encounters, and Rift Crypt checkpoint recovery. UI and debug tools should use these owners rather than duplicate gameplay state.
- The UI includes player/target frames, action bar, Inventory, Character, Talents, Spellbook, Journal, quest tracker, terrain-backed minimap and world map, a local chat hub, and an Escape game menu with searchable Settings. Spellbook lists the existing five combat definitions and assigns them to action bar slots; `PlayerCombat` owns and persists the slot IDs while the HUD reads assigned state. A reusable dark forged-metal uGUI style foundation supplies rounded charcoal surfaces, subtle metal edges, muted gold accents, and button states. Inventory is a compact right-side bag with smaller slots; the redundant inspection column is removed while item hover/comparison remains. Player and target frames and in-world nameplates use the darker style, and minimap/tracker widths align. Broader equipment layout, panel, and HUD restyling remain open issues. Settings persists keyboard and mouse-button overrides, including Mouse 4/5, Spellbook and auto run, plus camera controls, UI scale, screen shake, and video choices through PlayerPrefs. Auto run toggles forward movement and is cancelled by pressing backward. The chat has a metadata-driven developer command registry with generated help, autocomplete, movement/recovery/world/encounter/progression commands, and transient debug overlays. Godmode enables a rebindable cursor-teleport key plus left click; fly and noclip use the configured jump and strafe-left keys to rise and descend, or Ctrl to descend. Gameplay-changing commands run in the Editor or Development Builds. `docs/reference/UI_FUNCTIONALITY_FOLLOWUPS.md` records remaining interface and regression work.
- Outdoor maps use a `WorldMapDefinition` and **Phasebreak > Maps > Bake Current World Map**. V2 bakes separate terrain and road sprites from its four scene TerrainData tiles, their height and splat data, and scene vegetation density. The minimap samples a player-centered view of those same sprites without runtime baking. Terrain extents determine map bounds; scene QuestLocation transforms determine landmark positions and the map definition supplies four region labels. Player and quest markers remain live overlays. World-map landmarks support hover and selection details with distance, discovery, and matching quest context; the objective marker shows current quest progress. Dungeon and custom provider types are reserved but not implemented.
- PlayerPrefs stores progression, inventory/equipment, active specialization, all three talent allocations, quest progress, and selected UI positions. Existing builds with empty inventory and equipment reload as empty rather than receiving a new starter loadout; older build payloads without later fields remain readable. This is local prototype persistence, not an authoritative multiplayer save service.
- Quartermaster Orin in Northgate opens a five-offer Rift Marks shop. QuestJournal owns the balance and fixed prices, checks affordability, and saves deductions; purchases grant existing equipment through PlayerBuildSystem and its current inventory save.
- Corpse loot uses the highest remaining item rarity for a lightweight world cue: a subtle ground glint for Common, sparks from Uncommon upward, beams from Rare upward, and a second glint/beam for Mythic. The cue downgrades as items are taken and clears when the cache empties or despawns. A fresh V2 character equips a Common Frontier Blade; ordinary corpse drops exclude that blade and the boss artifact while keeping build-changing set pieces, Core, and Relics in the pool.
- Rift Crypt now has four authored encounters: a Zombie threshold, a Zombie/Puglin reliquary, a Puglin/Imp breach, and the Warden. Gates separate the fights, and each checkpoint becomes active when the player reaches it beyond the preceding gate.
- The Warden begins Bound with frontal slam and ground rupture, then becomes Unbound below 55% health, pauses to announce the transition, and alternates frontal slam with a larger convergence attack whose gold center is safe. The existing Mythic Heart grants build effects on the first cache claim; the build save records that claim alongside inventory so repeat dungeon clears cannot duplicate it.
- The lower-right utility bar has six compact buttons for Inventory, Character, Talents, Spellbook, Journal, and Map. The last two call the existing world-menu owner; all six retain their hotkeys and active-state feedback.
- The Character panel is a fixed compact window placed just left of the bag position. Its 16 equipment slots have in-bounds labels, the stat and set/modifier sections remain in the existing build UI, and selecting a slot shows its current item name or empty state without moving calculations out of `PlayerBuildSystem`.
- Item, equipped-item comparison, and action-bar tooltips use the shared charcoal/metal surface and warm headings. Item rarity still changes a restrained accent; Talent and map detail panels use the same surface family. Existing hover, comparison, and map context content remain in their current UI owners.
- The Talents specialization activation control sits inside the viewed specialization card; the active card remains marked. The Talent constellation and Game Menu/Settings panels use the shared dark metal surfaces and warm accent palette, alongside the already styled inventory, chat, map, tracker, and minimap.
- The combat action bar uses the same dark metal ready/blocked palette as the player and target frames. Its key, Energy cost, and current/maximum charge labels remain above the cooldown shade; insufficient Energy and zero charges have muted warning color.
- Starter Zombie quests match the displayed Risen Zombie identity from defeat events. Their map objectives target the authored broken-cart and ruined-shrine sites in V2; legacy fallback quest positions remain available in the older scene. Between quests, the tracker and map objective point to the next available quest giver, beginning with Warden Elira and ending with Scout Ilya.
- Berserker, Bulwark, and Riftblade are freely switchable through an explicit activation button in the Talents window. Each tree retains its own saved allocation and independently uses the level-derived point budget; inactive allocations remain saved but grant no talent effects. Switching while defeated or executing an ability is blocked. A successful switch clears transient talent buffs, recalculates build and ability modifiers, and reconciles charges without refilling Energy or granting free charges.
- Riftblade has a focused chain-kill slice: collision-respecting Phase Lunge movement, Phase Dash activating Rift Momentum, Energy-only Rift Execution, and one finishing-ability charge restored by Void Circuit or Lunge recovery equipment. Void Circuit also adds 35% Phase Lunge damage. Successful invested kills release the global cooldown and shorten Lunge recovery; ordinary and nonlethal attacks retain their cooldowns. Combat owns a three-second mobility-kill chain, presented as a temporary forged-metal toast with actual Energy/charge restoration, bounded trail/sparks, impact pitch and camera feedback. The first V2 encounter pocket is a deliberately spaced 12-zombie practice lane. Editor/Development commands `/riftbuild apply` and `/riftpack` equip the existing level-10 build and reset/travel to the lane; the loadout saves to the character and preserves owned gear and other specialization allocations.
- Editor authoring tools live under `Assets/Phasebreak/Editor`; MapMagic 2.1.19 is under `Assets/MapMagic`. Third-party attribution and license files remain with their assets.
